Online Shopping — Key Differences

  • Search results biassed toward promoted and own-label products
  • Substitutions at packing stage can override brand and organic preferences without consent
  • Delivery slots and minimum order values add cost and planning overhead
  • Product images often omit origin, certification, or full ingredient detail
  • Easier to compare price-per-unit but harder to assess freshness visually

In-Store Shopping — Key Differences

  • Store layout changes and aisle confusion disrupt efficient shopping
  • Promotional signage is prominent but often misleading on true value
  • No running total until checkout — budget control is entirely manual
  • Organic and origin labelling is small and inconsistently placed
  • Higher risk of impulse buys due to sensory environment and end-of-aisle displays

Key Friction Summary by Preference Category

Price

No live price comparison before or during the shop. Budget is managed reactively and only confirmed at checkout. Promotions are confusing and frequently mislead.

Brand

No proactive stock alerts for preferred brands. Substitution decisions are made on the spot without quality benchmarks. Loyalty rewards are easy to forget and miss.

Ingred

Specialist ingredients are stocked inconsistently. No substitute suggestions are offered at point of need. Missing items require an additional trip or separate order.

Misc

Organic, British, and ethical labels are small and inconsistent. There is no integrated filter for these values at any stage of the current shopping journey.
