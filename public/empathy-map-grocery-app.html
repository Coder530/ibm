Empathy Map

Easy Grocery Shopping App

🎓 Budget-Conscious Student

👁️ Sees

  • Price tags and promotional offers everywhere in physical stores
  • Friends and flatmates also trying to stretch their budgets
  • Social media posts about money-saving hacks, meal prep, and student deals
  • Multiple supermarkets and corner shops within walking or cycling distance
  • Long receipts at checkout with surprise totals
  • Other shoppers filling trolleys with branded products they cannot afford

👂 Hears

  • Flatmates recommending cheaper alternatives or specific stores
  • Family advice about which brands to trust versus avoid
  • Notifications from loyalty apps and discount services
  • News about rising food prices and the cost-of-living crisis
  • Peers talking about which supermarket is cheapest this week
  • Store staff announcing promotions over the tannoy

💭 Thinks & Feels

Thoughts

  • "I need to make sure I'm getting the best price before I commit to this shop"
  • "I don't want to visit three different stores just to save a few pounds"
  • "I always forget which brand I liked last time"
  • "What if there's a cheaper option two streets away?"
  • "I wish I could plan my whole week's shopping in one go"

Feelings

  • Anxious about overspending and running out of budget
  • Frustrated by the time wasted comparing prices manually
  • Relieved when a trusted brand is on offer
  • Satisfied when the final total comes in under budget
  • Overwhelmed by too many choices with no clear price ranking

🗣️ Says & Does

Says

  • "I always check if there's something cheaper before buying"
  • "I stick to certain brands because I know what I'm getting"
  • "I hate walking all the way there and finding it's sold out"
  • "If I could just sort everything by price I'd be sorted"

Does

  • Compares prices manually on multiple apps or websites before shopping
  • Favourites or screenshots products they want to buy again
  • Walks or cycles to nearby stores rather than travelling far
  • Creates mental or written shopping lists and ticks items off
  • Abandons a basket when the total gets too high
  • Returns to the same store out of habit, even if unaware of better options nearby

😣 Pains

  • No single place to compare prices across nearby stores
  • Forgetting which brands they liked and having to relearn each time
  • Wasted trips to stores that are out of stock or too expensive
  • Spending too much time planning instead of just shopping
  • Loyalty schemes that are hard to understand or require separate apps
  • Hidden costs such as delivery fees or minimum spends ruining the budget plan
  • App experiences that are cluttered, slow, or hard to navigate on a phone

🎉 Gains

  • Instantly knowing which nearby store has the lowest price for each item
  • A personalised favourites list that recalls preferred brands automatically
  • A single ranked view of products sorted cheapest first
  • An optimised shopping route or store recommendation based on current prices and proximity
  • Confidence that they are always getting the best deal without extra effort
  • A faster, less stressful shopping experience that fits around a busy student schedule
  • Reduced food waste through better planning and consistent brand choices

🎯 Summary

The budget-conscious student wants certainty that they are spending as little as possible, with minimal effort and wasted time. They value price transparency, proximity, and the ability to remember what worked before. The app must reduce friction at every step — from discovery to checkout — by sorting by price, surfacing nearby stores, and remembering their favourite brands so they never have to start from scratch.
